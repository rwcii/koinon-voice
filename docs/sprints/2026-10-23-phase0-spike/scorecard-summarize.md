# Scorecard — Summarize

Chunk 02's live check (definition of done, live check 1), re-run by chunk 03 with the Apple
engine, spoken timing and the maintainer's blind rating: every configured engine on the 20
synthetic fixtures in `internal/summarize/testdata/items/`. No real items were sent; the
maintainer has listed none. Chunk 02's first run, without speech or ratings, is this file at
6d1b90d.

The run was
`koinon-voice summarize --fixtures internal/summarize/testdata/items --speak --blind --rate 3`:
every summary was spoken, engines in a shuffled order per item with their names hidden, and
the maintainer rated listenability 1 to 5 right after hearing each summary of the first three
items (SYN-01 to SYN-03), 21 ratings. The other items were spoken and timed but not rated.

## Run

- Date: 2026-10-23, on an Apple silicon Mac (macOS 26.5, build 25F71), Go 1.27.3, one
  request at a time with a 90 s timeout and no client retries.
- Anthropic: `github.com/anthropics/anthropic-sdk-go` v1.84.0, streaming, effort `low`,
  thinking left at its default, no temperature (the API rejects it), no server-side
  fallback. Prices per million tokens, input/output: Haiku $0.10/$0.50, Sonnet $2/$10,
  Opus $4/$20.
- OpenAI: `gpt-6-luna` at $0.10/$0.50, priced from the configured rate.
- OpenRouter: `google/gemini-3.8-flash` with `provider.data_collection: "deny"`, so only
  hosts that neither retain nor train on prompts; the cost is the one OpenRouter reported.
- Local: oMLX 0.7.2 on `127.0.0.1:8000`, model `mlx-community/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-4bit`
  at revision `50d427756c6b1b2fe0c0a10f67fbda1fc8e82c1b` (2.1 GB), with no text leaving the
  machine. A 12B model was tried first and refused by oMLX's memory guard; the maintainer then
  set the size limit (chunk 02).
- Apple: the helper `koinon-voice-apple` 0.1.0 (chunk 03), Foundation Models framework 1.5.2,
  `SystemLanguageModel.default`, on the device with no text leaving it; the same instructions
  and prompt as the other engines. Usage is the helper's token count at no cost.
- Speech: every summary was spoken through the helper by `AVSpeechSynthesizer` with the voice
  `com.apple.voice.premium.en-US.Zoe`, which the maintainer chose after hearing Samantha
  (compact), Evan and Zoe (Enhanced), and Ava and Zoe (Premium). Each complete sentence goes
  to the synthesizer as soon as the engine has written it. An external engine's summary ends
  with "Summary by PROVIDER.", which is not timed.
- Timing: first spoken word is from the request to the engine until the first synthesized
  sample is rendered at the output; synthesis first audio is from the first sentence handed
  to the synthesizer to that sample. The output was a USB audio interface.
- Rating guide, given to the maintainer before the run: 5 clear on one hearing, nothing wrong
  or missing; 4 clear and correct but long or awkward; 3 the point comes through but needs a
  second hearing; 2 misleading or missing something important; 1 wrong or unusable. The
  voice is the same for every summary and is not rated.
- Cost of the whole run: $0.19.

## Reading the check

The correctness columns are deterministic: `ok` means every group of the answer key matched;
`missing` means one did not, which a paraphrase can cause; `none` means the item has no such
fact and the summary claims none; `invented` means the summary claims a recommendation or
deadline the item lacks. A `missing` is the maintainer's to confirm.

Answer keys were widened during the chunk where a correct summary was marked wrong (spoken
forms such as "seventeen", "v0 4 1" and "o m l x", and synonyms for the options). After
the run, SYN-05's second option also accepts "free form", which Haiku's correct "if you
don't, reviews stay free-form" did not match, and the check changed twice: a denial ("no
recommendation") counts only in its own sentence, since "without a new app build. Claude
recommends…" had hidden Qwen's invented recommendation in SYN-18; and in peer review it
must deny that very claim, with only filler words between, so "does not require a paid
licence and recommends Ava" is a claim. The recorded summaries were rescored with the final
check and keys, without a new provider run; only Haiku's SYN-05 row changed, to correct.


## Observations

- **Ratings.** Three per engine, so they separate engines only coarsely. OpenAI and
  OpenRouter's Gemini rated highest (4.7), then Opus and Qwen3-4B (4.3), Apple (3.7), Sonnet
  (3.3) and Haiku (3.0). Haiku and Sonnet were correct on all three rated items and still
  rated lowest; their summaries spell agent names out ("Codex koinon voice thirty-eight") and
  Sonnet's SYN-02 reads every measurement aloud. Apple's 2 is SYN-03, an FYI it turned into
  a request ("asks that the maintainer note").
- **First spoken word.** Qwen3-4B is the quickest (median 1.09 s), then Haiku (1.39 s),
  Sonnet (1.72 s), Apple (1.86 s), Opus (2.40 s), OpenAI (2.92 s) and Gemini (5.95 s). It is
  about the first sentence plus 0.48 s of synthesis, which hardly varies between engines.
- **Apple's on-device model** is free and private but the least correct, 12/20. It dropped
  options in SYN-06, SYN-11, SYN-16 and SYN-20, invented a deadline ("today") in SYN-05 and
  a recommendation in SYN-07, read a commit hash aloud (SYN-08), wrote a Markdown list that
  was spoken as "star" (SYN-14), and calls the listener "the maintainer" in 9 of 20. It
  answered every item, with no refusal or guardrail error.
- **Correctness moved between runs.** Opus and Gemini were 20/20 in chunk 02's run and are
  19/20 here: Opus's SYN-09 gives an option in its own words ("the M L X language model
  server") and is marked `missing`, and Gemini's SYN-12 says "there is no deadline" and is
  marked `invented`. OpenAI missed the ask in SYN-14 again and in SYN-07 too (18/20). They
  look like paraphrase or a check error and are the maintainer's to confirm.
- **The check marks a denial after its claim as a claim.** Apple's SYN-08 says "Deadline is
  not specified." and is marked `invented`: a denial counts only before the word it denies.
  The row is wrong for that field. The summaries were not rescored in this chunk; chunk 06
  can take the fix with the column for hashes and paths.
- Qwen3-4B is 14/20 as in chunk 02, with the same kinds of miss (options, an invented
  recommendation).
